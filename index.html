We built Pulse around one idea: your first visit shouldn't feel rushed.

Founded in 2017 by a group of Bhubaneswar-based doctors tired of 5-minute consultations, Pulse Clinic runs on longer appointment slots, real follow-ups, and specialists who actually talk to each other about your case.

Whether it's a routine check-up or ongoing care, we track your history so you never have to repeat your story from scratch.

  • Same-day appointments for urgent concerns
